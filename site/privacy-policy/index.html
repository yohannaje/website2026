Privacy Policy

Effective July 17, 2026

This policy explains how yhnn.com ("this site," "we," "us") handles information, including through its integration with the Pinterest API. This site is the personal website and portfolio of Yohanna (YHNN), a visual artist.

Who this covers

yhnn.com uses the Pinterest API to automatically publish the artist's own artwork to her own Pinterest account and boards. This is a personal-use integration: it connects a single Pinterest account (the artist's) and is not used to access, collect, or post on behalf of any other Pinterest user.

Information we collect

Browsing yhnn.com does not require an account, and the site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and user agent) for security and reliability, as is common for any website.

Through the Pinterest integration, we access:

  • An OAuth access token issued by Pinterest, used to authenticate API requests.
  • Basic Pinterest account and board information (such as account name and board IDs) needed to publish pins.
  • The artwork images and captions being published — content the artist already owns and controls.

How information is used

Data obtained through the Pinterest API is used solely to publish the artist's artwork to her own Pinterest boards. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to that publishing workflow.

Storage and security

The Pinterest access token is stored securely and is accessible only to the site owner. It is never displayed publicly, embedded in the website's source, or exposed to site visitors.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share Pinterest account data with third parties. Information is only exchanged with Pinterest itself, as required to use its API, and is subject to Pinterest's own Privacy Policy.

Artwork images displayed on this site are served through ImageKit, a content delivery service, which may log standard request data (such as IP address) to serve those images.

Revoking access

The Pinterest account connected to this integration can revoke its access at any time from Pinterest's own settings, under Apps. Revoking access immediately stops any further use of that account's data by this integration.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
